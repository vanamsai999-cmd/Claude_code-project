Axiom of Choice again
Axiom of Choice. (An equivalent form)
Let {Cλ : λ ∈ Λ} be an indexed family of nonempty subsets
of a set S. Then there is a function f ∈ F (Λ; S) with the
following property:
                 f (λ) ∈ Cλ for all λ ∈ Λ.
Such a map is called a choice function for {Cλ : λ ∈ Λ}.
