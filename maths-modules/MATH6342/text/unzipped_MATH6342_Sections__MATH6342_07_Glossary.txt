state-space model: linear system in terms of functions of time t, as in
ss(A, B, C, D);
summing junction: operator ⊕ for adding signals;
tap: operator ⊙ for making a signal go along two routes;
t: time, with t ∈ (0, ∞);
transfer function: T (s) multiplies the Laplace transform of the input to
give the Laplace transform of the output;
unit impulse function: δ0 the unit point mass at time t = 0, also
