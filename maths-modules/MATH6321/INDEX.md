# MATH6321 Mathematical Cryptography: file index

## Official (authoritative)
- Lecture notes: `MATH6321_Lecture_Notes.pdf` (latest copy). `MATH6321 Lecture Notes.pdf` and `MATH6321 Notes*.pdf` are near-identical copies.
  Chapters: 1 Classical cryptography (Caesar, substitution, Vigenère, one-time pad, index of coincidence),
  2 Mechanical cryptography (Enigma), 3 LFSRs, 4 Public key (Diffie–Hellman, RSA, RSA signatures, ElGamal), 5–7 later chapters.
- `MATH6321 Exam Information.pdf`: **exam format and examinable skills**. 2 hours, 5 multi-part questions
  (Ch 1 | Ch 2–3 | Ch 4 | Ch 5–6 | Ch 7). Proofs not examinable; mostly computational; calculator allowed;
  Useful Information sheet provided (e.g. ElGamal); "Attacking Enigma" non-examinable; some unseen parts.
- Coursework sheets: `MATH6321 Coursework 1.pdf` (5%, due 21 Oct), `MATH6321 Coursework 2.pdf` (5%, due 4 Nov), `MATH6321 Coursework 3 (1).pdf` (5%, due 18 Nov)
