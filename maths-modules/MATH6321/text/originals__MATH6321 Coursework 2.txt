  1. Habitants of Planet Squanch communicate secretly using a cipher machine that works identi-
     cally to the Enigma machine, except:

       • Their alphabet consists of only seven symbols.
       • They choose four rotors from six standard rotors with fixed wirings.
       • They use exactly two plugs in their plugboard.
       • They have a choice of three reflectors with fixed wirings.

     (a) (3 marks) How many ways are there to set up their machine in total?
     (b) (0 marks) Should we tell them that we have very good attacks against their encryption?

  2. A secret party is happening at one of the colleges on campus. Over the last few days a strange
     picture has been appearing everywhere:




     Luckily, you’ve been invited to the party and so have been told that:

       • The picture was encrypted using the first 56 bits of the output string s of an LFSR (using
         XOR on the pixels from left to right, top to bottom and the convention that white pixels
         are represented as 0’s and black pixels are represented as 1’s).
       • The LFSR has update rule an+6 = an+5 ⊕ an .
       • The initial fill used was a0 = (0, 1, 1, 0, 0, 1) ∈ F62 .

     (3 marks) Decrypt the picture and figure out where the party will be (you might find the
     following website useful: https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/about-us/colleges/).
  3. Consider the LFSR of length n = 5 with update rule an+5 = an+2 ⊕ an .

     (a) (3 marks) Write down the characteristic polynomial cb (x) ∈ F2 [x] of this LFSR and
         show that it is irreducible (You may assume the fact that the only irreducible quadratic
         polynomial over F2 is x2 + x + 1).
     (b) (1 mark) Hence deduce the period of the LFSR is Per(a0 ) = 31 for every initial fill
         a0 ∈ F52 \{0} (you may assume any result from the course).
