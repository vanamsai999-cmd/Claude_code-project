(a)

The recurrence an+5 = an+2 ⊕ an has tap vector

                                        b = (1, 0, 1, 0, 0).

By Definition 3.15, its characteristic polynomial is

                               cb (x) = x5 + x2 + 1 ∈ F2 [x].

Let f (x) = x5 + x2 + 1. If f were reducible, it would have an irreducible factor of degree
at most two. Indeed, a factorisation into at least two irreducible factors, all of degree at
least three, would have total degree at least six.
First,
                           f (0) = 1,        f (1) = 1 + 1 + 1 = 1
in F2 . Hence f has no root in F2 and therefore no linear factor.
The only irreducible quadratic over F2 is x2 + x + 1. Polynomial division gives

                            f (x) = (x2 + x + 1)(x3 + x2 ) + 1.

Since the remainder is nonzero, x2 + x + 1 does not divide f . Thus f has no irreducible
factor of degree one or two, so cb (x) = x5 + x2 + 1 is irreducible over F2 .
