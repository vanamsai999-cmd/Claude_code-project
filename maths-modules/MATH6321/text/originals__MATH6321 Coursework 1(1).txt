                             MATH6321 Coursework 1


This coursework is worth 5% of your mark for this module and should be handed in by 4pm on
Wednesday 21st October.

Crack the three ciphers below. Each plaintext is a quote from a well known book, and in each case
I have given you the type of cipher used. You may use any of the tools in the course to assist you
(or any others beyond the course as long as they are explained). In each case the key is related to
the corresponding book, but it is not necessary to use this fact to solve the ciphers.

You might find the following website useful for counting letter frequencies in strings of text:

                          https://www.dcode.fr/frequency-analysis.

Marks will be awarded based on the rigour and quality of your cryptanalysis, so try to give a full
explanation of what you are doing. Just handing in the plaintexts will only get you a small number
of marks. Marks are also available for partial attempts...so even if you don’t manage to break the
cipher you might still get marks for telling me what you tried!


  1. (2 marks) Caesar cipher:


     QB EIA I JZQOPB KWTL LIG QV IXZQT, IVL BPM KTWKSA EMZM ABZQSQVO BPQZBMMV.


  2. (3 marks) Monoalphabetic substitution cipher:


     A SG OSAPFY OSGAFASP WATK SFF OJPGQ JO QLEPLT WPATAICQ, SIR SG GYQLFO TKL SUTKJP
     JO S TPAOFAIC GJIJCPSMK UMJI TKL QUHBLET, AI WKAEK A SISFYZL JIL KUIRPLR SIR QAXTY
     QLMSPSTL EAMKLPQ, HUT A EJIOLQQ TKST TKAQ AQ LITAPLFY ILW TJ GL. TKL JHBLET JO TKJQL
     WKJ AIVLITLR TKL QYQTLG KSQ SMMSPLITFY HLLI TJ EJIELSF TKST TKLQL EKSPSETLPQ EJIVLY
     S GLQQSCL, SIR TJ CAVL TKL ARLS TKST TKLY SPL TKL GLPL PSIRJG QDLTEKLQ JO EKAFRPLI.


  3. (5 marks) Vigenère cipher:


     XTNIKIHVJWHTFNLXMVAAIWRALWENWJHTFNLXMVAAIWRALWEROWDFNQKSODQLLAJBZTAXMGUFF
     WDXSYVWHSZBOPSKPWTPFKZDFSMDXEWQLLAJBZTEGWUWOWQFRRVLMAIKGAIWRALWEJMSHOEWXA
     IXPLXTNIKIHVAWPSFVGUDRZCCEJA


     (Hint: the keyword length is between 3 and 6).
