## How to use these files (read this before answering a maths question)

1. Work out which module the question is about (module code, or topic: metrics/topology →
   6310, ciphers/RSA/LFSR/Enigma → 6321, state space/Laplace/transfer functions/feedback → 6342).
2. **Search `<module>/text/` first.** Every PDF has a plain-text copy there
   (`originals__<name>.txt`, or `unzipped_<zip>__<name>.txt` for PDFs inside zips).
   Use Grep on these instead of opening PDFs. Open the PDF in `originals/` only when you need
   diagrams or layout, or the text looks garbled.
3. **The official lecture notes are the authority** for notation, definitions and methods.
   Files written by Sai or by earlier AI chats (guides, cheat sheets, "worked solutions",
   mark schemes) are *not* authoritative: check them independently before reusing them.
4. LaTeX sources (`.tex`) for Sai's write-ups and guides are in `originals/` and `unzipped/`.
   Reuse their style and preamble when asked to produce new LaTeX/Overleaf documents.
